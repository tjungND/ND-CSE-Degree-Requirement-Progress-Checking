Claude Code appends here after the DGS answers a question; the next DGS reads this before
changing anything.

Decisions marked **DGS** were answered directly by the DGS. Decisions marked **default
(DGS-delegated)** are readings the DGS delegated on 2026-08-31 ("all other questions follow
